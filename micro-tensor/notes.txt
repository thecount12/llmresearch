Now that the absolute fundamentals are working, you are in a great position to start experimenting with the "better tensor-like algorithms" you mentioned earlier.
When you look at modern AI architectures, they are mostly built out of three simple operations layered on top of this matrix multiplication:
1. Element-wise operations (like adding a bias vector to every row or applying a ReLU activation).
2. Reductions (like finding the maximum value in a row or summing them up for a Softmax).
3. Reshaping (viewing the exact same contiguous memory array with different row/col dimensions without copying data).
